How to Measure and Size Anklets

Getting the right size is crucial — too tight and it's uncomfortable, too loose and it catches on things or falls off.

Step-by-Step Measuring

  1. Use a flexible measuring tape (dressmaker's tape)
  2. Wrap it around your ankle just above the ankle bone (the narrowest part)
  3. Note the measurement — this is your base ankle circumference
  4. Add 0.5-1 inch (1.5-2.5 cm) for a comfortable fit
Ankle Circumference Recommended Size Fit
8-9 inches (20-23 cm)9 inchesSnug but comfortable
9-10 inches (23-25 cm)10 inchesStandard fit
10-11 inches (25-28 cm)11 inchesLoose / relaxed fit

Pro tip for Filipinas: If you wear anklets with sandals or flip-flops that have an ankle strap, go slightly looser (add 1 inch instead of 0.5). If you wear mostly closed shoes or sneakers, a snugger fit works better.

Anklets and Sensitive Skin: What You Need to Know

If you have nickel allergies, you already know to be careful with earrings. But what about anklets?

The good news: ankle skin is generally less sensitive than earlobes. The bad news: in tropical humidity, sweat increases metal ion release from jewelry, which can trigger reactions even on less sensitive skin.

⚠️ Signs of nickel reaction from anklets:
• Red, itchy rash encircling the ankle
• Small bumps or blisters where the metal touches skin
• Dry, flaking skin that feels worse in hot weather
• Darkening or green staining of the skin (the latter is usually from copper, not nickel)

If you've had reactions to anklets in the past, switch to 316L surgical stainless steel or titanium. These materials are certified nickel-safe under REACH standards (nickel release under 0.5 μg/cm²/week).

FAQs About Anklets in the Philippines

What material is best for anklets in tropical climates?

316L surgical stainless steel is best for the Philippines. It's corrosion-resistant against salt water and sweat, and won't cause nickel allergy. PVD-coated 316L adds durable gold color.

Can I wear an anklet at the beach?

Yes — if it's 316L stainless steel. Non-waterproof materials will rust after a few beach trips. Rinse with fresh water after beach visits to extend lifespan.

How do I measure my ankle for an anklet?

Use a flexible measuring tape around your ankle bone, then add 0.5-1 inch for comfort. Standard size for most Filipinas: 9-10 inches.

Can sensitive ears wear anklets?

Yes — if the anklet is hypoallergenic. 316L or titanium anklets are safe for people with nickel allergies. The Isle Anklet is nickel-safe.

How do I clean my anklet?

Rinse with fresh water after beach/swimming, clean with mild soap and warm water, dry thoroughly. Avoid abrasive cleaners for PVD-coated anklets.
